Wholesale price list

2025 pricing

Effective 1 January 2025. All prices are per case, in Canadian dollars, before HST. We review pricing once a year in the autumn and give accounts at least 30 days’ notice before anything changes.

Volume tiers

Your tier is worked out per order, on the total number of cases in that order. You can mix any SKUs across any of the four product lines to reach a tier.

Shipping

Flat-rate freight by zone: $24 within the Golden Horseshoe, $38 elsewhere in Ontario, quoted separately for the Maritimes and the Prairies.

Shipping is free on orders over $750.

Payment terms

New accounts prepay by card or e-transfer for the first three orders. After that we move you to Net 30 on request, subject to a quick credit check.

Quoted prices hold for 30 days.

Questions about tiers, freight or lead times? Most of them are answered in the wholesale FAQ, or send us a note.
