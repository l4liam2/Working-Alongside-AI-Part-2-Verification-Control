## Selection constraints

Two rules were applied when choosing photographs, both to keep the site believable:

1. **No identifiable people.** A fictional company should not appear to staff itself with real,
   identifiable individuals.
2. **No third-party branding or foreign-market cues.** Several otherwise well-licensed candidates were
   rejected for showing competitor branding, euro or peso pricing, or non-Canadian signage — all of which
   would have undercut the premise rather than supported it.
