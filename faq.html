Opening an account

Do I need a business number to open a wholesale account?

Yes. We need your registered business name and your HST number. If you’re a sole proprietor operating under your own name that’s fine, we just need the number. Home bakers without a registered business are better served by our sample-pack web store.

How long does account approval take?

Usually two business days, sometimes same day if you send everything at once. We’ll email you an account number and a price sheet. Nothing else is required before your first order.

How do I hear about new products and restocks?

The monthly newsletter is the main channel. It goes out to the customer list — about 2,400 addresses now — with new SKUs, restock dates, seasonal items and any price changes coming up. Tick the box on the contact form and you’re on it.

Orders and minimums

Is there a minimum order?

Yes. The wholesale minimum is 6 cases per order, mixed however you like across the four product lines. First orders on a new account have a minimum of 8 cases, which is mostly so we can be sure the freight economics work for both of us. Below that, the sample-pack store is the better option.

Can I mix product lines to reach a volume tier?

Yes, and most accounts do. Tiers count total cases on the order, not cases of any one SKU. Six cases of cups and four of boxes puts you in Tier 2 exactly the same as ten cases of cups.

Do you break cases?

No. Case counts are on the products page and they’re firm. If you need a smaller quantity to trial something, order a sample pack instead.

How do I reorder?

Email orders@marnwoodpackaging.example with your account number and the SKUs, or phone it in. There’s no online ordering portal for wholesale accounts yet — it’s on the list.

Can I get custom printing?

All four lines, one or two colours, water-based inks. Minimum run is 25 cases per SKU for a custom print, and setup takes four to six weeks from artwork approval.

Shipping and pickup

What does shipping cost?

Flat rate by zone: $24 within the Golden Horseshoe, $38 elsewhere in Ontario, $42 to Montreal and the surrounding region. Maritimes and Prairies go LTL and we quote those per order. Orders over $750 ship free.

How quickly do orders go out?

One to two business days from Hamilton, two to three from Montreal while the new warehouse gets its rhythm. Add transit on top: next day for most of southern Ontario, one to two days for Montreal.

Do you ship across Canada?

Ontario and Quebec are the core. We ship to the Maritimes and the Prairies on LTL freight, but the cost stops making sense for most small accounts. British Columbia we’ll quote but rarely win.

Do you ship to the United States?

No. Cross-border paperwork on compostable food-contact packaging is more than a 19-person company wants to take on right now.

Can I pick up my order?

Yes, from Barton Street in Hamilton, weekdays 8:00 to 16:30. Montreal pickup is by appointment only for now — give the office a day’s notice and we’ll have it on the dock.

Which warehouse will my order ship from?

Quebec accounts ship from the Montreal warehouse on rue Saint-Patrick, which opened in April. Everyone else ships from Hamilton. If Montreal is short on a SKU we’ll fill from Hamilton and eat the difference in freight rather than split the order.

Something arrived damaged. What now?

Photograph the carton before you unpack any further and email it the same day. We’ll ship a replacement case on the next run and deal with the carrier ourselves. You don’t need to return the damaged goods.

Product and certification

Are the cups actually compostable?

The Hearth cups and lids and the Fieldpress sheets are BPI certified compostable, which means they break down in an industrial composting facility. They will not usefully break down in a backyard pile, and we’d rather say so than let you tell your customers otherwise. The Ridgeline boxes and Corner Store bags are recyclable, not compostable.

Can my customers put them in the green bin?

Depends entirely on the municipality, and it changes. Hamilton and Toronto currently do not accept compostable foodservice packaging in the green bin. Some smaller Ontario programs do. Check your own city’s list before you print anything on a sign.

Are your products PFAS-free?

Yes, across all four lines. Nothing we stock has intentionally added fluorochemicals, and the Fieldpress papers are third-party tested to below 20 ppm total fluorine.

Billing and changes

What are your payment terms?

Card or e-transfer for the first three orders on a new account, then Net 30 on request after a quick credit check. We don’t charge interest on late invoices, we just phone you.

How often do prices change?

Once a year. We review in the autumn and the new list takes effect on 1 January. Accounts get at least 30 days’ notice, usually through the newsletter. The current list is the 2026 one.
