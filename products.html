Everything below is stocked year-round in Hamilton and, since April, in Montreal. Case counts are firm — we don’t break cases. Certification documents and food-contact declarations are available on request for any SKU on this page.

Hearth Series Hot Cups

Double-wall paper hot cups. The insulating layer means no sleeve for most drinks, which is one less thing to stock. Lined with a water-based dispersion coating rather than PLA, so they tolerate a hotter pour and don’t soften on a long shift.

Ridgeline Kraft Boxes

Recyclable kraft board boxes for bakery counters, pastry boxes and takeout mains. Grease-resistant without a fluorochemical treatment, which does mean they will eventually wet out under something very saucy — pair the large with a Fieldpress liner if you’re boxing anything wet.

Corner Store Bags

Plain kraft paper bags for counter service. Nothing clever about them. The handled sizes use a twisted paper handle glued under a reinforcing patch, which is the part that usually fails on cheaper bags.

Custom printing

All four lines can be printed with water-based inks in one or two colours. Set-up runs four to six weeks from artwork approval, and there’s a minimum run of 25 cases per SKU for a custom print. Send artwork as vector through the contact form and we’ll quote it.
