How wholesale works

Send us your details

Business name, HST number and where you’re located. The contact form covers it.

We set up your account

Usually two business days. You’ll get a price sheet and an account number for reorders.

Order and we ship

Out of Hamilton for Ontario, out of Montreal for Quebec. Most orders leave within two business days.

New this year: our Montreal warehouse

We opened a second warehouse on rue Saint-Patrick in Montreal in April, and signed our first Quebec accounts shortly after. It cuts transit to most of the island down to a single day and means Quebec customers are no longer waiting on a truck from Hamilton.

Get the monthly email

About once a month we send a short newsletter: new products, restock dates, price changes, and the occasional note about what’s going on in the warehouse. Roughly 2,400 people are on the customer list now, most of them cafe and bakery owners.

Drop your address in the contact form and we’ll add you.

Not ready for a case?

We keep a small web store for sample packs. You get a handful of each product line, the current spec sheets, and a credit toward your first wholesale order if you decide to go ahead. It’s the easiest way to check whether our 12 oz lid actually fits the cups you already have.
