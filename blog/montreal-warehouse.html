We opened a warehouse in Montreal

21 April 2026

As of last Monday we have a second warehouse. It’s at 2250 rue Saint-Patrick in Montreal, it has about a third of the floor space of the Hamilton building, and there are three people running it.

This has been coming for a while. We’ve had Quebec enquiries almost since we started, and for four years the answer was some version of “we can ship it to you, but.” The but was freight. A skid of cups leaving Barton Street on a good day reached Montreal in two days. On a bad week — construction, weather, the 401 doing what the 401 does — it was four or five, and a cafe that has run out of 12 oz cups does not care whose fault that is.

What changes for Quebec accounts

Orders from Quebec accounts now pick and ship out of Montreal rather than Hamilton. For most addresses on the island that’s next-day delivery instead of a three-to-five day gamble. Freight to Montreal and the surrounding region is now a flat $42 zone rather than a per-order quote.

Pickup is available from the Saint-Patrick dock, but by appointment for the moment. The three-person crew is still learning where everything lives and we’d rather not have you standing in the cold while somebody finds the liners.

First accounts signed

We signed our first Quebec accounts in the two weeks after opening — a handful of cafes in Mile End and Verdun, a bakery in Saint-Henri, and a small restaurant group we’d been talking to since last summer. That group’s buyer had been quietly telling us for a year that the freight situation was the only thing stopping them, which turned out to be the most useful market research we’ve ever got for free.

What doesn’t change

Same four product lines, same case counts, same price list. Montreal stocks everything Hamilton stocks. If Montreal is short on a SKU we’ll fill from Hamilton and absorb the freight difference rather than split your order across two shipments and two arrival dates.

Anyone in Quebec who’s been waiting on us: now’s a reasonable time to get in touch. Details on how accounts work are in the FAQ.
