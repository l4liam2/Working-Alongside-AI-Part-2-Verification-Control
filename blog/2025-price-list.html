The 2025 price list is up

4 March 2025

New price list, effective now. It went out to the customer list on the newsletter last week, but a few of you asked for it somewhere you could bookmark, so here it is: the 2025 wholesale price list.

The short version: board costs moved less over the winter than anyone in the trade expected. We had been braced for something ugly after the autumn, and it didn’t arrive. So the adjustment this year is a modest one, and it isn’t even across the board.

What went up

The Hearth cups took most of it. The water-based lining we use costs meaningfully more than the PLA coating on a commodity cup, and that gap widened again this year. Lids moved with the cups.

Ridgeline boxes went up a little on the large size only. The vented large is the one that eats the most board, and it was underpriced relative to the medium in a way our bookkeeper had been pointing at for two years.

What didn’t

Corner Store bags are essentially flat. Recycled kraft has been stable and we passed that through. Fieldpress sheets moved a percent or so, nothing you’ll feel on a case.

Tiers are unchanged

Same three tiers, same thresholds, still calculated per order rather than annually. We keep being asked to switch to annual volume pricing and we keep deciding not to, because it would quietly punish the seasonal accounts — the patio places and the farm-stand bakeries — who are exactly the people we started this for.

Anything that looks wrong on your sheet, phone the office. Ivy still does this by hand and it is not unheard of for a number to come out sideways.
